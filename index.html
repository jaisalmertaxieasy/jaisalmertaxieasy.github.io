What we drive for

Four kinds of trips, built around how people actually move through Jaisalmer.

Local sightseeing

Half-day or full-day city hire covering Jaisalmer Fort, Patwon Ki Haveli, Gadisar Lake and the old bazaars, at a pace you set — waiting time at each stop is built in, not billed as extra.

Outstation, one-way or round trip

Jodhpur, Jaipur, Udaipur, Bikaner and beyond. Pay for a one-way drop instead of a return fare you don't need, or book a round trip with the same driver and car throughout.

Airport & railway transfers

Flight and train timings tracked, so a late arrival doesn't mean an empty pickup point. Meet-and-greet with a name board on request.

Desert camp & safari transport

Transfers to Sam and Khuri for sunset dune visits, camel safaris and overnight desert camps, timed around sunset and the camp's own pickup schedule.
